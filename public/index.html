Emergency service categories

Know what each assistance button is for.

The ES 24/7 client app presents clear assistance categories. Use the appropriate option for the situation and provide accurate information when prompted.

Medical assistance

AMBULANCE

For a medical emergency or serious injury requiring urgent medical attention. Call the appropriate ambulance emergency number immediately when urgent care is needed.

Police assistance

POLICE

For situations requiring police assistance, such as suspected criminal activity or an immediate threat. Where a crime is in progress or anyone is in immediate danger, contact SAPS directly as well.

Fire response

FIRE FIGHTER

For fires, smoke, or suspected fire-related danger. Move to safety and contact the appropriate fire and rescue emergency service immediately.

Roadside recovery

TOW TRUCK

For vehicle breakdowns or road incidents where towing or roadside recovery may be needed. Move away from traffic and follow road-safety precautions.

ES 24/7 is a technology and connection platform. Selecting an assistance category does not itself guarantee that a public emergency authority or responder has been dispatched. For life-threatening emergencies, contact the relevant emergency service directly.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you apply.

What is ES 24/7?

ES 24/7 is a platform that helps people discover participating security companies serving their area and submit an application directly to a selected provider.

Does ES 24/7 provide security services itself?

The website connects prospective clients with participating security companies. Each company is responsible for its own services, coverage, membership decisions and client communications.

How do I find a company?

Select your province, enter your town or city and suburb or area, then search. Companies whose registered coverage text matches your selected area will be listed.

Where does my application go?

Your application is sent directly to the registered email address of the company you select. It is not sent to a central ES 24/7 application inbox.

Will I see a company's telephone number?

No. This public directory displays the company's name, coverage information and registered application email address. Company telephone numbers are not displayed.

Does submitting an application activate my account?

No. The selected company must review your application and communicate its acceptance, membership terms and account setup steps.

Does pressing a panic button guarantee an emergency response?

No platform can guarantee the availability or arrival time of responders. For immediate danger, contact the relevant emergency authority directly and do not wait for an app notification.

What if no company appears for my area?

Check the spelling and try a nearby suburb or town. If no participating company is listed, coverage may not currently be registered for that location.

How do I get the client app?

After the company accepts your application, it will provide the client onboarding instructions and app access information.
