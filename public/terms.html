Important information for clients.

These terms explain the website, application process and relationship with participating security companies.

1. About the platform

ES 24/7 provides an online directory and application channel intended to connect prospective clients with participating security companies. ES 24/7 is not, solely by virtue of this website, the security provider, police service, ambulance service, fire department or towing operator.

2. Company selection and coverage

Area listings are based on coverage information supplied and maintained by participating companies. A listing does not guarantee that a company can accept an application, provide service at every address in the area, or respond at a particular time.

3. Applications

You must provide accurate, current and complete information. When you submit an application, you instruct the website to send the information to the registered email address of the company you selected. The selected company is responsible for reviewing the application and contacting you.

4. No automatic membership

Submitting an application does not create a client account, activate a subscription, establish a service contract or guarantee acceptance. Membership, fees, coverage, equipment and service conditions must be confirmed directly with the selected company.

5. Emergency use and limitations

The website is not an emergency call centre. Do not rely on this website or an app notification as a replacement for contacting emergency authorities directly. Response availability, dispatch decisions and arrival times depend on the relevant provider and public emergency services.

6. Client responsibilities

You are responsible for safeguarding your login credentials, keeping contact and location information accurate, using the app responsibly and promptly notifying your provider if your device, account or contact details change.

7. Privacy and information handling

Application information is routed to the company you select for the purpose of considering your application. Do not submit information about another person unless you are authorised to do so. The selected company will handle information it receives under its own applicable privacy obligations and notices.

8. Misuse

False applications, abusive submissions, malicious activity or knowingly false emergency alerts may result in refusal of service or further action where appropriate.

9. Changes

Website features, participating companies and these terms may be updated. The version published on this page applies to use of the website from its publication.

10. Questions about an application

For questions about a submitted application, contact the company you selected using the email address displayed in its listing or the reply you receive from that company.
