Emergency services

Know which assistance option fits the situation.

The ES 24/7 client app presents clear assistance categories. Choose the appropriate option and follow the on-screen prompts.

Police assistance

POLICE

For suspected criminal activity or an immediate threat. If a crime is in progress or someone is in immediate danger, contact SAPS directly as well.

Fire response

FIRE FIGHTER

For fires, smoke or suspected fire-related danger. Move to safety and contact the appropriate fire and rescue emergency service immediately.

Medical assistance

AMBULANCE

For a medical emergency or serious injury requiring urgent medical attention. Contact emergency medical services immediately when urgent care is needed.

Roadside recovery

TOW TRUCK

For vehicle breakdowns or road incidents where towing or roadside recovery may be needed. Move away from traffic and follow road-safety precautions.

Selecting an assistance category does not itself guarantee that a public emergency authority or responder has been dispatched. For life-threatening emergencies, contact the relevant emergency service directly.
