FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before choosing a company and applying.

What is ES 24/7?

ES 24/7 is a platform that helps people discover participating security companies serving their area and submit an application directly to a selected provider.

Does ES 24/7 provide security services itself?

The platform connects prospective clients with participating security companies. Each company is responsible for its own services, coverage, membership decisions and client communications.

How do I find a company?

Select your province, enter your town or city and suburb or area, then search. Companies whose registered coverage includes the selected area will be listed.

Where does my application go?

Your application is sent directly to the registered email address of the company you select. It is not sent to a central ES 24/7 application inbox.

Will I see the company's telephone number?

No. The public directory displays the company's name, coverage information and registered application email address. Company telephone numbers are not displayed.

Does submitting an application activate my account?

No. The selected company must review your application and communicate its acceptance, membership terms and account setup steps.

Does pressing a panic button guarantee an emergency response?

No platform can guarantee the availability or arrival time of responders. For immediate danger, contact the relevant emergency authority directly.

How do I get the client app?

After the company accepts your application, it will provide the client onboarding instructions and app access information.
