About ES 24/7

A simpler way to connect with a security company serving your area.

ES 24/7 helps people discover participating security companies operating in their location and apply directly to the provider they choose.

DISCOVER

Find local coverage

Choose a province, town and area to see participating providers whose coverage includes your location.

CHOOSE

Select your company

Review the company name, listed service coverage and its application email address.

APPLY

Send your application directly

Your application is sent to the selected company's registered email address, not to a central ES 24/7 inbox.

ES 24/7 connects prospective clients with participating providers. Each company remains responsible for its own services, coverage, membership decisions and emergency response arrangements.
