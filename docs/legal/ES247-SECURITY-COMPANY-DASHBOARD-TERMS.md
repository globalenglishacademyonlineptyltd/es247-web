The security company operates independently and is responsible for its own representations, advertising, promises, client contracts, staff, control-room operations, staffing levels, monitoring procedures, operational decisions, dispatching, response times, equipment, communications and emergency-response services.

Where available, platform logs may help investigate transmission attempts, timestamps, errors and acknowledgements. An attempted transmission is not necessarily proof that the company's dashboard received an alert; the parties must distinguish between platform receipt, transmission attempt and confirmed receipt.
